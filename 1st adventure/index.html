Rules

Registers. Every 2 seconds the sprouts at the base fire a fresh register, all qubits in |0⟩. It passes your plant slots one by one, then reaches the measurement gate at x = 5.

Measurement gate (10 HP). It measures every qubit in the Z basis (outcomes 0, 1) or the X basis (outcomes +, −), drawn from the Born rule. Measured qubits are classical from then on. At 0 HP the gate disappears: registers stay quantum all the way to x = 15, and classical zombies walk to the base. Losing the gate does not end the game.

Classical zombies (0, 1, +, −). They live beyond the gate. Each needs two matching measured outcomes in its lane. A zombie that reaches the gate breaks on it and costs 1 gate HP.

Quantum zombies. They carry a qubit state on their card and walk straight through the gate into the lab zone. An unmeasured qubit that meets one is tested against that state once: it matches with probability |⟨zombie|qubit⟩|² (Born rule). A match costs the zombie a heart and uses the qubit up. If the states are different they do not interact: the qubit passes straight through, and an exactly opposite (orthogonal) state is not changed at all. Two matches dissolve the zombie, so the closer your state is to the card, the faster it falls.

Rotations (level B). RY(θ)|0⟩ = cos(θ/2)|0⟩ + sin(θ/2)|1⟩ sets the weights. RZ(φ) then adds the relative phase e^(iφ) to |1⟩. RX(θ) mixes both: RX(θ)|0⟩ = cos(θ/2)|0⟩ − i sin(θ/2)|1⟩.

The entangled boss (level C). It is a Bell state such as (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2. It shoves through the gate (2 HP) and then tests whole, unbroken, unmeasured registers once each, as soon as they have passed all your planted gates. A matching register is used up; a different one passes straight through, and the boss never heals. Any product state |a⟩|b⟩ matches a Bell state with probability at most 1/2, so the boss falls only when your matches prove a match rate above 1/2 (the 3σ lower bound shown as the green bar on its card). That makes it an entanglement witness: only an entangled register (try H and then CNOT) can beat it. If it reaches the base you lose 3 HP and it walks back to the far end; your matches so far still count.

Game over when the base loses all 10 HP.
